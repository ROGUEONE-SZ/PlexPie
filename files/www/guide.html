Build and install

From a box of parts to music playing, in about an hour. You only need a computer for the first part.

1. What you need

PartNotes
Raspberry Pi 4 or 52 GB or more. A Pi 4 with 4 GB is plenty.
Official power supplyCheap phone chargers cause random freezes.
microSD card32 GB or larger, "A1" or "A2" rated (SanDisk, Samsung).
TouchscreenThe official 7" Touch Display or Touch Display 2, or any HDMI touchscreen.
USB DAC (recommended)From about $50 (SMSL, Topping, FiiO). Sounds far better than the headphone jack.
CableRCA (DAC to receiver) or 3.5 mm (DAC to powered speakers).
AccountsPlex (free). Optional: Apple Music, Spotify Premium, Home Assistant.

2. Connect the hardware (power unplugged)

  1. Screen: the official display's ribbon cable goes in the Pi's DISPLAY connector. An HDMI screen uses HDMI plus its USB cable (for touch).
  2. Network: a network cable is most reliable. Wi-Fi works too.
  3. Sound: pick one of these:

3. Put Raspberry Pi OS on the memory card

  1. On your computer, install Raspberry Pi Imager from raspberrypi.com/software and open it.
  2. Choose Device: your Pi. Choose OS: Raspberry Pi OS (64-bit). Choose Storage: the memory card.
  3. Click Next, then Edit Settings: set a hostname (e.g. plexpie), a username and password (write them down), your Wi-Fi if you're not using a cable, and your time zone.
  4. On the Services tab, turn on Enable SSH with password authentication.
  5. Save › Yes › Yes. When it's done, put the card in the Pi and plug in the power. The first start takes 2 to 3 minutes.

4. Install PlexPie

  1. On your computer, open Terminal (Mac: ⌘ Space, type Terminal) or PowerShell (Windows: Start, type PowerShell).
  2. Type ssh pi@plexpie.local (your username and hostname), press Enter, type yes, then your password. Nothing shows while you type the password; that's normal.
  3. Paste this line and press Enter: 
    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ROGUEONE-SZ/plexamp-raspberry-pi-install/main/get.sh | bash
  4. Answer the short questions (press Enter to accept a suggestion): player name, which apps you want, screen sleep.
  5. When it asks for a claim code: open plex.tv/claim on any device, copy the code, paste it, press Enter, type the player name, and press Ctrl+C when Plexamp says it's ready.
  6. It takes 15 to 30 minutes. When it's done, the screen shows the PlexPie home screen.
  7. Screen standing up instead of lying down (the Touch Display 2 starts in portrait)? Settings › Screen › Rotation › Right or Left.
Can't connect? Use the Pi's number address instead of plexpie.local. Your router's list of devices shows it, e.g. ssh pi@192.168.1.42.

Plexamp

Your own music library, in full quality.

  1. Plexamp is linked to your Plex account during the install (the claim code step). Settings › Configuration › Plexamp shows which account.
  2. On the screen, tap Plexamp and pick music, or
  3. On your phone or tablet, open the Plexamp app, tap the speaker icon and pick your player. Your phone becomes the remote.
Music plays but the screen spins or shows no "now playing"? Settings › Configuration › Plexamp › Reset the Plexamp screen, then sign in to Plex once on the screen. This happens after the player's network address changes.
Linked to the wrong account, or not linked? Run the installer again from a computer (Build and install, step 4). It asks for a new claim code.

Apple Music

The Apple Music web player, right on the screen.

  1. Settings › Configuration › Apple Music: switch it on and tap Save.
  2. On the home screen, tap Apple Music, then Sign In (top right).
  3. Enter your Apple ID. If you get a code on your iPhone, type it on the screen.
  4. You stay signed in. PlexPie never sees or stores your password.
Want to play from your iPhone instead? Use AirPlay: it works with Apple Music and every other app.

To sign out: Settings › Configuration › Apple Music › Sign out.

AirPlay

Play anything from an iPhone, iPad or Mac.

  1. Make sure your device is on the same Wi-Fi as the player.
  2. iPhone or iPad: open Control Center (swipe down from the top-right corner), tap the AirPlay icon on the music tile, and pick your player.
  3. Mac: click the AirPlay or Control Center icon in the menu bar and pick your player.
  4. The screen shows the cover, title and a visualizer. Volume follows your device.
Don't see it? Check Settings › Configuration › AirPlay is on, and that your device isn't on a guest Wi-Fi.

Spotify

Two ways: pick the player from the Spotify app (Premium), or browse Spotify on the screen.

Spotify Connect (from your phone, tablet or computer)

  1. Settings › Configuration › Spotify: switch on Spotify Connect and tap Save.
  2. In the Spotify app, start a song, tap the devices icon (bottom left) and pick your player.

Spotify web player (on the screen)

  1. Switch on Spotify web player and tap Save.
  2. Tap the Spotify tile on the home screen and log in once.

Bluetooth

Any phone, tablet or computer can play to it, even a guest's Android phone.

  1. On the screen, tap Bluetooth › Pair a device (or Settings › Configuration › Bluetooth › Pair). The player is visible for 2 minutes.
  2. On your device, open Settings › Bluetooth (Mac: System Settings › Bluetooth) and tap your player.
  3. Confirm on your device if it asks. No PIN is needed.
  4. Play music. Next time it connects by itself: just pick it in your device's Bluetooth or AirPlay/output list.

To remove a device: Settings › Configuration › Bluetooth › Forget.

Music Assistant

If you run Music Assistant (usually inside Home Assistant), the player becomes one of its speakers.

  1. Settings › Configuration › Music Assistant: switch it on.
  2. Server address: the computer that runs Music Assistant, e.g. homeassistant.local or 192.168.1.20.
  3. Web address: the Music Assistant page, usually the same address with :8095, e.g. http://homeassistant.local:8095.
  4. Tap Save. In Music Assistant, open Settings › Players: your player appears there. Turn it on.
The dot next to Server turns green when the player can reach Music Assistant.

Caldera Music

Caldera Music is a free Plex Labs player. With it, the player shows up in Plexamp's speaker list and can play in sync with other Caldera speakers around the house.

  1. On the Pi, run bash configure.sh and answer yes to Add Caldera Music, then sudo bash install.sh.
  2. The installer downloads Caldera Music and asks you to sign in to Plex once. Follow what it shows on the screen.
  3. In Plexamp on your phone, tablet or computer, tap the speaker icon and pick your player (Caldera). To play in more rooms, add your other Caldera speakers there too.
  4. When it plays, the screen shows Playing via Caldera. Pause all and the volume work as usual.
You'll see the player twice in Plexamp: your player is Plexamp on the screen, (Caldera) is the multi-room one. Switch Caldera off any time in Settings › Configuration › Caldera Music. It shares the sound output with the other apps; for Caldera alone on the DAC, set CALDERA_DEVICE="caldera" in plexpie.conf and run the installer again.

Home Assistant

Control the player from Home Assistant, show a dashboard on the screen, and add your own buttons.

1. Connect (MQTT)

  1. In Home Assistant, install the Mosquitto broker add-on (Settings › Add-ons) and the MQTT integration, if you haven't.
  2. Create a Home Assistant user for the player (Settings › People › Users), e.g. plexpie.
  3. On the player: Settings › Configuration › Home Assistant. Enter the Broker (your Home Assistant address), port 1883, and that username and password. Tap Save.
  4. In Home Assistant, the player appears under Settings › Devices & services › MQTT with now playing, volume, source, sleep timer, screen, brightness, pause/play, the PlexPie and Raspberry Pi OS updates, restart and shut down.

2. Dashboard tile on the screen

  1. Switch on Home Assistant tile and enter the Address, e.g. http://homeassistant.local:8123.
  2. Optional: make a small dashboard just for this screen and enter its name under Dashboard, e.g. gym-screen.
  3. Tap the tile once and sign in to Home Assistant on the screen.

3. Launcher buttons

  1. Under Launcher buttons, type names separated by commas, e.g. Gym lights, Sound, and tap Save.
  2. Each one appears on the home screen and as a switch in Home Assistant. Use it in an automation, e.g. when "Gym lights" turns on, turn on the lights.

4. Alerts on the screen

Call notify.send_message on the player's Screen alert entity with a message, e.g. "Someone's at the door". Add a camera picture with the MQTT topic plexpie/<player>/alert/set.

Workout timer

Big, readable, with beeps. Keeps the screen on while it runs.

  1. Settings › Configuration › Workout timer: switch it on.
  2. On the home screen, tap Timer. Pick Intervals, Countdown or Stopwatch.
  3. For intervals, set Work, Rest, Rounds and Ready with − and + (hold to go faster), then Start.
  4. It beeps for the last 3 seconds of each part. Music keeps playing.

Equalizer

Shape the sound for your room, speakers or headphones.

  1. Settings › Sound: switch on Equalizer.
  2. Pick a Preset: Less bass, More bass, Loudness (for quiet listening), Vocal, Soft treble, or Custom (5 sliders).
  3. The curve shows what changes. Tap Save.

Headphones or room correction

  1. For headphones, open autoeq.app, pick your model, choose Equalizer APO and copy the parametric EQ.
  2. For a room, measure with REW and export the filters as Equalizer APO text.
  3. Pick the preset Pasted filters and paste the text.
With the equalizer off, lossless music plays untouched.

Remote access

Change settings from any phone, tablet or computer on your Wi-Fi.

  1. On the player: Settings › Remote access. Note the Address and PIN, or scan the code with your camera.
  2. On your device, open the address in Safari, Chrome or any browser.
  3. Enter the PIN. You only do this once per device.
  4. Tip: add it to your home screen (Safari: Share › Add to Home Screen) so it opens like an app.

Every device you sign in shows under Registered devices, with when it was last used. Tap Sign out to remove one, or New PIN on the player to sign them all out.

Backup and restore

One small file with all your settings, for a new memory card or a second player.

Make a backup

  1. On the player: Settings › Backup. Scan the code with your phone or tablet camera (or open Settings on a registered device).
  2. Tap Download. On an iPhone or iPad, choose Save to Files (iCloud Drive is a good place).

Restore

  1. Install PlexPie on the new card (Build and install).
  2. Open Settings › Backup from a registered device, tap Choose file and pick the backup.
  3. The player reinstalls itself with your settings (about a minute). Sign in to Apple Music, Spotify and Home Assistant on the screen again.
The backup includes your Home Assistant password. Keep it somewhere private.

Troubleshooting

Most problems go away with one of the quick fixes. The full list, with commands, is in TROUBLESHOOTING on GitHub.

Quick fixes, in this order

  1. Wait two minutes after the player starts or updates: the apps take a moment.
  2. Settings › System › Restart PlexPie (a few seconds).
  3. Settings › System › Reboot (about a minute).
  4. Unplug the power for 10 seconds if the screen doesn't respond.
  5. Settings › Updates: many problems are fixed in newer versions.
ProblemTry this
Home screen too big, cut off on the rightIt got pinch-zoomed. Update to 2.9.2 or newer and reboot; until then, Restart PlexPie.
Plexamp screen blank or "loading"Update to 2.9.3 or newer (it reloads itself), or Restart PlexPie.
Album art spins, music slow to startYour router blocks plex.direct (DNS rebind protection). Allow plex.direct in its DNS settings; on pfSense add private-domain: "plex.direct" under Services › DNS Resolver › Custom options.
Music plays, but the Plexamp screen spins or shows no now playingThe player's address changed. Settings › Configuration › Plexamp › Reset the Plexamp screen, then sign in to Plex once on the screen.
Update stuck, or the version never changesTap Update again. On 2.8.x, update once by hand from a computer (see TROUBLESHOOTING on GitHub).
Screen jumps to Bluetooth by itselfA paired phone reconnected. Update to 2.9.2 or newer, or Forget the device.
Phone can't find the player over BluetoothTap Pair a device first: it's visible for 2 minutes.
AirPlay or Spotify doesn't list the playerSame network (not guest Wi-Fi); turn on mDNS/Bonjour forwarding in the router.
Home Assistant shows "unavailable"Wait two minutes; check the green dot in Configuration › Home Assistant.
Receiver or smart plug stops respondingGive it a fixed address in the router; unplug the receiver for a minute.
Songs skip or stutterRemove old players with the same name on plex.tv (Account › Authorized Devices) and pick the player again in Plexamp. Then check power (official supply) and Wi-Fi; the report's playback health section shows both.
Screen in portrait (or upside down), but you want landscapeSettings › Screen › Rotation: Right or Left. Automatic follows the Raspberry Pi desktop's setting. Touch turns with it.
Screen blackTap it (asleep). Still black: unplug for 10 seconds.
Caldera isn't in Plexamp's speaker listSettings › Configuration › Caldera Music: check it's on and signed in. Not signed in: run the installer again from a computer (ssh -t). Caldera needs a 64-bit Raspberry Pi OS on a Pi 4 or 5.
Caldera plays, but other apps go silent (or the other way round)Caldera has the DAC to itself. Set CALDERA_DEVICE="auto" in plexpie.conf, run the installer again and reboot.
Report for help: from a computer, run
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ROGUEONE-SZ/plexamp-raspberry-pi-install/main/tools/diag.sh | ssh USER@ADDRESS bash
and attach the output to an issue on GitHub.

Screen sharing

Mirror the player's screen on your Mac, iPad or iPhone and use it as if you were standing in front of it.

  1. On the player: Settings › System › switch on Screen sharing and tap Save. It shows the address, like vnc://192.168.1.42.
  2. Mac: in Finder choose Go › Connect to Server, type the address and click Connect. Enter your Remote access PIN as the password. Clicks work as taps.
  3. iPad or iPhone: install the free RealVNC Viewer app, tap +, enter the address without vnc://, and use your PIN as the password. Tap and swipe on the picture.

Getting around without swiping

  • Home tab: while screen sharing (or with a mouse plugged in), a small tab with a house appears on the left edge. Click it to go back to the home screen.
  • Keyboard: press the Home key, or Esc twice.
  • Mouse: the mouse's Back button also goes home.
Screen sharing only works on your home network, and the password is your PIN: New PIN under Remote access changes it too. Switch it off when you don't need it.

Desktop and command line

Use the Pi for something else for a while. Music keeps playing.

  1. Settings › System › Exit. The screen switches to the Raspberry Pi desktop (or the command line on Raspberry Pi OS Lite, which needs a keyboard).
  2. To come back, use any of these: the Back to PlexPie icon on the desktop, type plexpie in a terminal, tap Back to PlexPie in Settings on your phone, or restart the player.
With a keyboard, Ctrl + Alt + F2 opens a text login without leaving PlexPie; Ctrl + Alt + F1 goes back.
